# Privacy Policy for CodeMateX

*Last updated: August 2026*

Welcome to **CodeMateX** ("the App"). Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use CodeMateX on Android.

---

## 1. Zero Cloud Data Collection

CodeMateX is built from the ground up as a **100% on-device AI application**. 
- **Your Code and Prompts**: Any source code, questions, prompts, and chat sessions you submit are processed entirely on your physical device using local on-device runtimes (such as Google LiteRT-LM).
- **No Cloud Inference**: Your queries and code snippets are never transmitted to external servers, cloud AI APIs, or third-party inference backends.
- **No Account Required**: The App does not require registration, login, email addresses, or usernames.

---

## 2. Local Device Storage

All chat history, topic configurations, and downloaded on-device model weights are stored locally in the App's sandboxed private storage (`Room Database` and local file system on your device).
- You have full control to inspect, export, or permanently delete individual chat sessions or all saved data at any time from within the App.
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

---

## 3. Network Usage

The App requests the `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` permissions exclusively for the following purpose:
- Downloading public, open-source on-device AI model weights (such as Gemma) directly from verified model repositories when explicitly requested by the user in the Model Manager.

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or remote diagnostic telemetry.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Links

The App does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. If the App provides links to external documentation or source repositories, visiting those third-party sites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date in this document.
